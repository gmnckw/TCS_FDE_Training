# Data export, deletion, and GDPR

**Article ID:** KB-105
**Tags:** export, gdpr, deletion, dsar, privacy, cancellation

## Self-serve export

Any customer on any plan can export their workspace from
**Settings -> Data -> Export workspace**. The job runs asynchronously and emails
a download link. Formats: JSON (complete) or CSV (tabular records only).

Typical completion times: under 10 minutes for Free and Pro, up to 6 hours for
large Enterprise workspaces. Links expire after 7 days.

## Erasure requests (GDPR Article 17)

A request to delete personal data is a **Data Subject Access Request**. Support
must **not** action deletion directly. The required handling is:

1. Acknowledge in writing within **72 hours**.
2. Route the ticket to the **Privacy team** with the tag `dsar`.
3. Tell the customer the statutory response deadline is **30 days** from receipt.
4. Advise them to export their data first - deletion is irreversible.

Never confirm that data has been deleted. Only the Privacy team makes that
confirmation, and only after the erasure job has completed.

## Cancellation combined with erasure

Treat these as two separate work items. Cancellation is a billing action and can
be handled in-channel. Erasure always routes to Privacy. Answer both in one
reply, but open the Privacy routing regardless of the billing outcome.

See [KB-101](billing-refunds.md) for whether a refund accompanies the
cancellation - monthly plans are not prorated.
