# Meals and entertainment

**Article ID:** EX-201
**Tags:** meals, business meal, alcohol, attendees, entertainment, client

## Business meals

A business meal is any meal with a work purpose: with a client, or an internal team meal.

- The limit is **$75 per person per meal**. The limit for a meal is the number of people
  (including the employee) multiplied by $75.
- For a meal with two or more people the claim must **name the attendees** (or say "me plus N
  guests" with their organisation) and give the **business purpose**. If either is missing, the
  claim is `needs_info`.

## Alcohol

Alcohol is **never** reimbursable.

- If the receipt itemises the alcohol, take it out first and reimburse the rest. The per-person
  limit is applied **after** the alcohol has been removed.
- If alcohol was clearly bought but the receipt does not itemise it, the claim is `needs_info`.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment means events, shows or sports tickets, not meals. It needs a manager
pre-approval of type `client_entertainment` when the claim is **over $200** (see EX-206 for what
makes a pre-approval valid). Reimburse up to the amount the pre-approval allows.

## Meals while travelling

An employee who is away overnight and claims the per diem (EX-202) does not claim their own meals
separately. See EX-202.
