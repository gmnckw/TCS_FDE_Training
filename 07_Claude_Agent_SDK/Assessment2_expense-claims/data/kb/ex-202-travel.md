# Travel

**Article ID:** EX-202
**Tags:** travel, flights, hotel, per diem, ground transport, parking

## Flights

- Fly **economy** on flights shorter than 6 hours.
- **Business class** is allowed only on flights of 6 hours or longer, and only with a valid pre-approval
  of type `travel_business_class` (EX-206). Reimburse up to the amount the pre-approval allows.

## Hotels

- The limit is **$220 per night**.
- In the **high-cost cities** the limit is **$320 per night**: London, New York, San Francisco,
  Singapore and Zurich.
- Convert to USD first (EX-206). For each night, reimburse the lower of the actual rate and the limit.
  A receipt or folio is required.

## Per diem

- The per diem is **$60 per full day** away overnight, covering meals and incidentals.
- No receipts are needed for per-diem days.
- A meal claimed separately for a day that is already covered by the per diem is **not reimbursable**.
  Reimburse the per diem and nothing for that meal.

## Ground transport

Taxis, ride share and trains for business travel are reimbursed at actual cost. A receipt is required
(EX-204).

## Parking

Airport parking is reimbursed at actual cost up to **$25 per day**, with a receipt.
