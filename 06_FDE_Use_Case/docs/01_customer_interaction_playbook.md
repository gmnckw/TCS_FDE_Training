# Customer Interaction Playbook for Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)

An FDE bridges the gap between customer business pain and core engineering execution. Below is how the FDE engages Meridian Health & Claims stakeholders across the engagement.

---

## Stakeholder Map & Communication Cadence

| Stakeholder Persona | Role at Meridian | Primary Concern | FDE Interaction Cadence & Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VP of Claims Operations** | Executive Sponsor | Cycle time, SLA breaches, headcount cost | Weekly 30-min readout; align on KPIs & POC sign-off criteria |
| **Lead Claims Adjusters (SMEs)** | Domain Experts | Edge cases, policy exceptions, false approvals | Daily 30-min working sessions; co-author Golden Dataset |
| **Enterprise Data / IT Architect** | Technical Gatekeeper | PII security, schema contracts, API stability | Twice-weekly sync; lock `target_schema.json` & webhook specs |
| **Compliance & SIU Lead** | Risk & Audit | Fraud leakage, regulatory audit trails | Gate review before shadow-mode & production cutover |

---

## Phase-by-Phase Customer Interaction Script

### Stage 1: Discovery & Empathy Interview (Days 1–2)
* **Objective:** Uncover unstated tribal knowledge that isn't written in PDFs.
* **What the FDE Asks SMEs:**
  - *"Walk me through the last 5 claims that took more than 20 minutes to process. What made them hard?"*
  - *"When an adjuster sees `pol8821` instead of `POL-8821`, do they fix it manually or reject the claim?"*
  - *"Which rule violation gets someone fired if auto-approved by mistake?"* (Reveals the hard `> ₹1,00,000` and SIU gates).
* **Claude Cowork Action:** Feed meeting notes + sample CSV into Cowork to immediately surface discrepancies between what SMEs *say* and what the data *shows*.

### Stage 2: Co-Design & Rapid Prototyping Workshop (Days 3–5)
* **Objective:** Show working software on real data within 48 hours to build credibility.
* **How the FDE Presents:**
  - Never show slideware alone. Open Claude Cowork live, run the normalization script on Meridian's actual export, and highlight caught duplicates (`CLM-1002`, `CLM-1012`).
  - Ask SMEs to challenge edge cases live (e.g., `CLM-1011` at `₹1,00,001`).

### Stage 3: Shadow-Mode Calibration & Trust Building (Days 6–8)
* **Objective:** Prove safety before touching production payouts.
* **How the FDE Interacts:**
  - Share the **Confusion Matrix & Diff Report** generated by `evaluate_pipeline.py`.
  - Walk through every mismatch with the Lead Adjuster: *"Our pipeline flagged `CLM-1008` as `AWAITING_DOCS` because `PA-XXXX` was missing—do you agree, or is there an override table?"*

### Stage 4: POC Sign-Off & Operational Handoff (Days 9–10)
* **Objective:** Transition ownership so Meridian is self-sufficient and feed product insights back to R&D.
* **Deliverables Handed to Customer:**
  - Validated pipeline code + JSON schema contract.
  - `operator_runbook.md` tailored for Level-1 Ops support.
  - Formal POC Sign-Off scorecard demonstrating ≥98% routing accuracy and 0% false auto-approvals.
